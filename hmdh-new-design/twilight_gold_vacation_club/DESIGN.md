## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a premier hospitality and luxury membership visual presence. It captures the atmosphere of twilight resort evenings, exclusive private lounges, and sun-drenched coastal escapes. Targeted at discerning travelers and families seeking curated, tier-based holiday memberships, the aesthetic blends polished corporate modern structure with high-end editorial warmth.

The overall style combines **Minimalism** with deep **Tonal Layers** and **Luminous Amber Accents**:
- Dark-mode twilight header, hero sections, and navigation panels create immersive evening allure.
- Daylight-clean content canvases provide effortless legibility for packages, itineraries, and tiered club perks.
- Rich golden-amber micro-interactions and call-to-actions evoke warmth, prestige, and seamless VIP service.

## Colors

The palette balances intense twilight darkness against warm, sun-kissed metallic ambers and clear ocean blues:

- **Primary (`#061325` & `#05131c`)**: The foundational brand identity tone. Used across hero headers, footers, dark luxury backdrops, and primary typography in light areas.
- **Secondary (`#e59819` & `#ff9900`)**: The radiant amber and gold accents used for conversion buttons, tier badges, star ratings, and key highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#73b7dd`)**: Soft sky azure representing open skies and crystal resort pools, utilized for subtle active states, informational markers, and secondary icons.
- **Neutral (`#f8fafc` to `#ffffff`)**: Crisp, immaculate surfaces that provide high-contrast legibility for membership breakdowns, booking modules, and resort specifications.
- **Emerald Accent (`#25d366`)**: Reserved for live concierge communication and verified booking triggers.

## Typography

Typography is clean, modern, and open, leaning on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels to project contemporary luxury, approachable authority, and crystal-clear readability.

- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold and extra-bold weights (`700` and `800`) with tighter letter tracking (-0.02em) to provide refined architectural weight against scenic resort photography.
- **Body:** Generous line heights (1.5–1.6) guarantee effortless browsing across dense resort itineraries, room features, and legal terms.
- **Labels & Badges:** Rendered with elevated weights (`600` and `700`) and slight uppercase letter spacing (+0.05em) for tier status chips, pricing tags, and navigation anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model implements a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed around high-impact hospitality media and structured tier comparisons:

- **Desktop (1024px and above):** 12-column grid with a max-width container of `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` outer padding.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer canvas padding.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` margins. Navigation collapses to a deep twilight sheet.

Spacing rhythm relies on an 8pt baseline scale. Generous vertical section offsets (`space-xl` and larger multiples) ensure destinations and membership packages receive visual breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages high-depth twilight backdrops paired with soft, tinted ambient shadows for content containers:

- **Level 1 (Subtle Cards & Lists):** Flat background `#ffffff` with a soft twilight ambient shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(6, 19, 37, 0.06)`. Border is a gossamer line of `1px solid rgba(6, 19, 37, 0.08)`.
- **Level 2 (Featured Membership & Interactive Cards):** `0 12px 32px rgba(6, 19, 37, 0.12)`, accentuating interactive resort previews and active pricing tiers.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Concierge Floating Elements):** `0 24px 64px rgba(6, 19, 37, 0.28)`, ensuring absolute contrast and priority over dark or light backdrops.
- **Dark Mode Elevation:** Within dark twilight canvases (`#061325`), elevated cards transition to `#0E314C` surface containers with faint gold or cyan ghost borders (`1px solid rgba(229, 152, 25, 0.2)` or `rgba(115, 183, 221, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced rounded architecture (level `2`):
- Standard cards, interactive inputs, and modal sheets use a default corner radius of `0.5rem` (8px).
- Larger media cards, resort image galleries, and pricing containers feature `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Interactive pills, status badges, and action buttons employ full capsule borders (`9999px`) to maintain friendly luxury and tactile elegance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Call to Action / Pay Now / Check Plans):** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), background colored in vibrant amber (`#e59819`), transitioning to deep gold (`#c87216`) on hover. Typography is bold white with soft amber drop shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(229, 152, 25, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Twilight-navy fill (`#061325`) with crisp white text, or an outline version with `1.5px` border in `#061325` (in light mode) or `#e59819` (in dark twilight mode).
- **Utility / Floating Action:** Circular emerald floating button (`#25d366`) positioned bottom-left/right for direct WhatsApp support.

### Membership & Destination Cards
- **Structure:** Clean white background (`#ffffff`), `rounded-lg` corners, covered with a top photography slot (16:9 ratio).
- **Badge Accents:** Top-left floating chips denoting "Exclusive Offer", "10 Glorious Years", or membership tier (Gold, Platinum) in `#ff9900` with dark-navy label text.
- **Hover Motion:** Subtle translateY (-4px) with elevated shadow shift to `Level 2`.

### Inputs & Booking Controls
- **Fields:** Subtle grey-slate backgrounds (`#f1f5f9`), `0.5rem` rounded corners, `1px` border in `rgba(6, 19, 37, 0.12)`.
- **Focus State:** Amber outline halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 152, 25, 0.2)`) and border turning `#e59819`.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding.
- Light amber tints (`rgba(229, 152, 25, 0.12)`) paired with `#c87216` typography for amenities and offers.

### Modal Windows & Notification Dialogs
- High-contrast promotional modals centered with dark backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` with `rgba(5, 19, 28, 0.75)` overlay).
- Card container features champagne-gold gradient top highlights and crisp close icons.
